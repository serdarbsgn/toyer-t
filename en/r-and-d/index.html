Design Center

A team expanding the boundaries of engineering with Ministry-approved R&D center status since 2018.

Who We Are

Our Engineering Team

ATM has had a Design Center approved by the Ministry of Industry and Technology since 2018. In this department, intensive studies are carried out on both Machinery Manufacturing and Heat Transfer.

We actively use government supports in our work and closely follow advanced machine technologies in the world.

Our qualified staff continuously work on new machines and solutions, advancing their experience and knowledge every day. We are in constant cooperation with Technical High Schools and Universities; we continuously produce solutions for the companies we work with in the areas of improvement and cost reduction.

Our colleagues consist of Mechanical Engineers, Design Engineers, Mechatronics and IT engineers, and experienced technical staff.
