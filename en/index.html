TOYER / ATM Machine Manufacturing Group

Engineering Power in Machine Manufacturing

TOYER is a brand born within ATM Heat Transfer Industry and manufactures the machines used in ATM's production. We started machine manufacturing in 1987 with Resistance Welding Machines.

While evolving to meet the growing need for machines, dies and special automation solutions, we enhanced our capabilities through KOSGEB and TÜBİTAK projects. The success of our machines stems from directly addressing our own production line needs and being perfected through years of use.

We have an experienced team of machine designers, metallurgy, mechatronics and software engineers. With our Design Center approved by the Turkish Ministry of Industry and Technology, collaborating with universities and holding 18 Industrial Property Rights, we continuously develop solutions.

Full Product Catalog

  • Welding Machines

    Fully automatic resistance welding systems — 300 wires in a single stroke. From condenser manufacturing to automotive.

  • Tube Forming Machines

    Serpentine, 3-axis CNC, hairpin and burr-free cutting systems. Steel, copper, aluminium.

  • Tube End Forming

    Tube end flaring, flanging and forming machines. High precision.

  • Tube Forming Dies

    Inspection jigs and process dies manufactured with CNC milling, turning and 3D printing.

  • Tube Inspection Fixtures

    Dimensional inspection fixtures for formed tubes. Automatic approval label feature.

  • Helium Leak Test Machines

    Helium leak testing with zero-defect tolerance. Chamber test, sniffer and recovery.

  • Heat Exchanger Tube Expansion

    Pneumatic and servo-controlled tube expander machines for fin heat exchanger production.

  • Refrigerator Dryer Filter Machines

    Filter dryer automatic dosing, silica gel filling and sealing machines.

  • AI-Integrated Brazing

    Vision processing + deep learning quality-controlled fully automatic brazing cell.

  • Automatic Wire Coil Decoilers

    Wire pulling from spool, controlled tension and tangle-free decoiling machines.

  • Spool Handling and Tilting Hangers

    Lifting, transporting and tilting hangers for wire and tube spools up to 500 kg.

  • Titanium Sheet Superplastic Forming

    Apache helicopter heat shield sheet forming system in collaboration with TAI.

  • Quick Couplings

    1.2–8 mm copper/steel tube quick couplings. Helium test precision.
